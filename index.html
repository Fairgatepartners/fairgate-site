FAQ

Questions about pay-per-call.

What is pay-per-call?

Pay-per-call is a marketing model where a business pays for each qualified inbound phone call from a potential customer, rather than paying for clicks, impressions, or form leads. Fairgate Partners delivers live calls from homeowners directly to home-service companies.

What counts as a qualified call?

A qualified call is one that meets the terms agreed on before calls start, such as the caller's location, the service they need, and a minimum call length. You are billed only for calls that meet those terms.

Which businesses does Fairgate Partners work with?

Fairgate Partners works with home-service companies, such as trades that serve homeowners with repairs, installations, and emergency services.

Can I control where and when I receive calls?

Yes. You choose the ZIP codes you serve, the hours you take calls, and a daily cap on how many calls you receive, so call volume matches your team's capacity.

Are the calls recorded?

Yes. Every call is tracked and recorded, so you can hear exactly what you paid for.

Do you guarantee booked jobs?

No. Fairgate Partners delivers live inbound calls from homeowners who are looking for service. Whether a call becomes a booked job depends on your pricing, availability, and how your team handles the call. You are billed only for calls that meet the qualification terms agreed upfront, and every call is recorded so you can review it.

How are calls sourced?

Calls come from consumers who have requested service, in compliance with applicable telemarketing laws, including the TCPA.
