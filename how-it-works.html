How pay-per-call works

A homeowner calls, your phone rings, and you pay only if the call lasts. Here’s the whole process.

Step 1

A quick call to set terms

We talk for 10 to 15 minutes about where you work, your hours, your daily cap and your price per call. It all goes in a one-page agreement.

Step 2

Prepay a small block

You prepay for a small batch of calls. Calls start once payment clears and pause when the balance runs out.

Step 3

Answer and close

Homeowners call, your phone rings, and your team takes it from there. Answer fast: calls go to whoever picks up first.

What you pay for, and what you don’t.

Billable

  • A homeowner from your area, during your hours
  • Connected to your phone
  • Lasted past the minimum in your agreement

Not billable

  • Calls shorter than the minimum
  • Calls from outside your area or hours
  • The same caller again within 30 days
  • Wrong numbers, spam and robocalls, once disputed

Disputes are simple.

If a call wasn’t what it should have been, email us within 7 days with the date, time and caller number. We listen to the recording and reply within 3 business days. Approved disputes go back on your balance as credit.

A call isn’t a bad call just because the homeowner was price shopping or didn’t book. You’re paying for a real conversation with a real homeowner.

What is pay-per-call?

You pay for live phone calls from homeowners who need your service, instead of paying for clicks or form leads. The call rings straight to your phone.

What counts as a billable call?

A call that connects to you and lasts past the minimum length in your agreement, often somewhere from 60 to 120 seconds. Calls from outside your area or hours, repeat callers and short hang-ups are not billed.

Are calls exclusive?

No. A call can ring more than one company and connects to whoever answers first. The faster your team picks up, the more calls you get.

Can I control where and when I get calls?

Yes. You choose the ZIP codes or cities, the hours you take calls and a daily cap, so volume matches what your crew can handle.

Are calls recorded?

Yes. Calls are recorded so you can hear what you paid for and so disputes can be checked.

What if I get a bad call?

Tell us within 7 days. We review the recording, and approved disputes are credited to your balance.

How do I pay?

You prepay for a small block of calls. Calls pause when the balance runs out, so you never get billed for more than you bought.

Do you guarantee booked jobs?

No. We deliver live calls from homeowners looking for service. Whether a call becomes a job depends on your pricing, availability and how your team handles the call.

Is there a long contract?

No. You can start with a small test and stop anytime. Any unused prepaid balance is refunded.

Ready to test it in your area?

Tell us where you work and we’ll set up a 10-minute call.
