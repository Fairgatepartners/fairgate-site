About Fairgate Partners

A small pay-per-call company in Los Angeles, connecting homeowners who need work done with the contractors who do it.

What we do

Fairgate Partners LLC works with marketing partners who run ads homeowners respond to, like search ads and local listings. When a homeowner calls, we route the call live to a contractor in their area who has agreed to take it.

We keep it simple on purpose: clear terms up front, prepaid blocks instead of long contracts, and recorded calls so everyone can see what happened.

How calls are sourced

Calls come from homeowners who chose to call. We don’t use robocalls, and we don’t cold-call homeowners and transfer them to you.

Our marketing partners must follow telemarketing and consumer-contact laws, including the TCPA.

Who you’ll deal with

Theo Brown runs Fairgate Partners. When you call or email, you reach him directly, not a call center.
